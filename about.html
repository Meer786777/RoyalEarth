About Royal Earth

Landscaping & Maintenance Company in Dubai, UAE

Royal Earth Landscape & Gardening Work & Maintenance Co. LLC is a Dubai-based company specializing in professional landscaping and maintenance services. We transform outdoor spaces into beautiful, functional, and lasting environments for residential and commercial properties.

Who We Are

Royal Earth is a team of experienced professionals offering landscaping and maintenance solutions throughout Dubai. Our expertise covers natural and artificial grass care, palm tree services, pool maintenance, marble & tile work, and interlock pavement installation.

We combine technical expertise, premium materials, and meticulous attention to detail to create outdoor spaces that are visually stunning, functional, and durable.

Every project is executed with precision, professionalism, and care for your property.
